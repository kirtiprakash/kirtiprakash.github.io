Local document processing

Files are processed in browser memory. The app has no document-upload endpoint and does not write claims to browser storage. Save and restore an AES-GCM encrypted case only when you choose. Libraries and optional OCR language models come from pinned third-party CDNs and can be cached for later offline use; this is not a guarantee of fully offline operation. Use only with university approval for sensitive records. Check the evidence and employer-specific requirements before submitting.

How ClaimWeave works

  1. Enter the claim context. Add your name, trip and optional BW reference.
  2. Upload everything together. PDFs and images become evidence pages; MSG and EML are parsed into readable email pages.
  3. Check each expense. Set the expense name, original amount, original currency and GBP claim. Assign every file to an expense or to “General approvals”.
  4. Review the warnings. Amount extraction is only a suggestion. Scanned images do not have automatic OCR. Email attachments can be included automatically.
  5. Export one PDF. Review it locally before uploading it to BW. No claim content is intentionally sent to this website's server. Pinned third-party libraries still need an online first load.

Limitations: Encrypted PDFs, unusual Outlook MSG variants, image-only receipt text, non-image email attachments and very large packs may require conversion first. A failed MSG/EML import is excluded rather than silently omitted from a supposed complete pack.
