<section class="profile-section" aria-labelledby="background-heading">
  <div class="section-header">
    <p class="section-kicker">Background</p>
    <h2 id="background-heading">Computer science, biology and quantitative microscopy.</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="two-column-copy">
    <p>
      Kirti Prakash trained across computer science and biology, with an MSc from Aalto University and a PhD
      from Heidelberg University. His doctoral research on DNA and chromatin architecture was nominated as an
      outstanding PhD thesis and later published by Springer Nature as <em>Chromatin Architecture</em>.
    </p>
    <p>
      His research develops AI, machine learning and computational modelling approaches for high-dimensional
      scientific datasets, especially large-scale imaging, digital pathology, spatial transcriptomics and
      super-resolution microscopy. He is an elected Fellow of the Royal Microscopical Society.
    </p>
  </div>
</section>
