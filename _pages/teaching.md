My teaching is interdisciplinary and example-driven. I connect core ideas in programming, statistics, machine learning, mathematical modelling, and image analysis to real scientific and biomedical problems.

## Selected teaching

- **ESRIC Summer School** — invited lecture and hands-on practical workshop, 2024
- **Hands-on Super-resolution Workshop**, University of Leicester — invited lecturer, 2024
- **Transforming Health Services with AI**, Coventry University London — invited lecturer, 2023
- **Modelling of Biological Systems**, Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz — lecturer, 2017
- **Advanced Seminar: Biophysics of Genome Structure**, Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz — lecturer, 2015
- **Biomedical Optics and Medical Physics**, Mainz and Heidelberg — lecturer and teaching assistant, 2014–2015

## Mentoring and leadership

I have supervised MSc, PhD, summer, and undergraduate researchers in machine learning, microscopy, digital pathology, and genome biology. At the Institute of Cancer Research, I led an AI/ML team developing digital pathology pipelines. At TU Delft, I supported the supervision of three MSc and three PhD researchers.

## Public engagement

Through the **Microscopy Wonderland** programme at the National Physical Laboratory, I delivered ten talks to 60 schools in five days, reaching approximately 4,500 pupils. I have also contributed to international summer schools, open-science initiatives, and training for researchers adapting advanced microscopy in lower-resource settings.
