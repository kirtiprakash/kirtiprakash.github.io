My work on chromosome biology began with Boolean modelling of the histone code and developed into direct super-resolution imaging of DNA and epigenetic modifications.

I characterised nanoscale patterns along meiotic chromosomes, contributed to the discovery of reversible chromatin compaction under stress, and continue to develop an imaging-guided view of genome structure that connects molecular modifications to three-dimensional organisation.
